Market comparison colors

Pinnacle is the hidden benchmark. DraftKings and FanDuel are the automatically displayed retail books; Check can evaluate a manually entered offer from any sportsbook.

BlueBetter line than Pinnacle.
GreenBetter price than Pinnacle.
PurpleBoth line and price are better.
RedThe line or price is worse than Pinnacle.
No colorSame line and price as Pinnacle.

Spread comparison is side-aware: -7 is better than -10 for the same favorite, and +10 is better than +7 for the same underdog. For totals, a lower Over line or higher Under line is better. Moneyline compares price only.
